# Ideas & Future Directions

## Potato Farm Modernization (2026-05-25)
Potential vertical to sell into — farms are tech-laggard:
- Precision IoT soil sensors (moisture, pH, nitrogen)
- Drone scouting with computer vision for blight/pest detection
- GPS-guided micro-tractors for precision planting
- Traceability QR codes on bags — premium pricing, retailers starting to require
- Direct contracts with distributors to lock in seasonal pricing
- Solar for irrigation pumps

**Key insight:** Most small farms still operate like 1985. Gap between available tech and what's being used is large — and mostly affordable now.
